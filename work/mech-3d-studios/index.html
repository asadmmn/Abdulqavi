An e-commerce platform for 3D products

A Laravel and Vue.js commerce experience covering product discovery, checkout, inventory and administration.

LaravelVue.jsE-commerceAdmin panel

Overview

Mech 3D Studios is an e-commerce platform for 3D products, bringing the storefront and operational tools into one connected application.

The challenge

A specialist product store needs more than a catalogue. Customers need a clear route from product discovery to checkout, while the business needs ways to manage inventory and store operations.

Approach & decisions

The implementation covers the storefront, checkout, inventory management and an administration panel, with Laravel and Vue.js as the core application technologies.

Technology

Laravel · Vue.js · E-commerce · Admin panel

Outcome

The project scope brings the customer shopping journey and key store-management tasks together in one e-commerce platform.
