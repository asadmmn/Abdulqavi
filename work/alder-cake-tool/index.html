A focused WooCommerce store for baking tools

A WordPress and WooCommerce store with tailored product pages, upsell flows and a streamlined checkout.

Overview

Alder Cake Tool is an online store for baking and cake tools. The project focuses on product presentation and a straightforward purchasing journey.

The challenge

A specialist store needs product pages that help customers understand what they are buying, alongside a checkout that does not add unnecessary friction.

Approach & decisions

The work includes customised product pages, relevant upsell flows and a streamlined WooCommerce checkout, keeping the store experience centred on product discovery and purchase.

Technology

WordPress · WooCommerce · PHP · E-commerce

Outcome

The delivered scope gives the store tailored product presentation and a more considered checkout journey, while retaining WooCommerce as the store platform.
